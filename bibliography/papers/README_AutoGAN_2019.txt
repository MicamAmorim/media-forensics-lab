MFLab usage note:
The v0.8 implementation is an independent clean-room reproduction of the spectral preprocessing and frequency-band idea described in the paper and exposed by the official repository. No upstream source file or pretrained weight is bundled. The method is treated as GAN-upsampling artifact analysis, not as a universal AI detector. A negative result does not exclude GAN generation and does not address diffusion generators in general.
